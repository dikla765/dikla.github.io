### The Worm Is in the Root: On the Security of IoT Provisioning Protocols and PMF in Wi-Fi Devices
- **Authors**: Lucien Dikla Ngueleo (1), Kevin Jiokeng (2), and Valeria Loscri (1)
- **Affiliations**:  
  (1) Inria Lille--Nord Europe, Lille, France ;(2) LIX, CNRS, École polytechnique, Institut Polytechnique de Paris, Palaiseau, France
- **Abstract**:  
  We conduct an in-depth security analysis of IoT provisioning protocols and Protected Management Frames (PMF) in Wi-Fi smart-home devices. We propose a systematic methodology to study the exploitability of provisioning weaknesses and introduce `PMFInspect`, an automated tool for evaluating PMF support and robustness against forged management frames. Our evaluation of 25 IoT devices from 10 vendors shows that 4/10 vendors rely on weak provisioning protocols, 3/10 do not implement PMF, and 18/25 devices are vulnerable to combined attacks exploiting both weaknesses.

- **Conference**: ACM WiSec 2026

- **Link**:  [Pdf](https://hal.science/hal-05615219v1/document)
